ABOUT US

Welcome to Student Hub Portal!


Student Hub Portal is a simple web-based platform designed to provide students with easy access to
important academic information in one place. The portal helps students stay updated with programmes,
announcements, student services, and general college-related information.

Our aim is to make it easier for students to navigate different sections of the college website
without confusion. The portal is designed with a clean and user-friendly interface so that students
can quickly find the information they need.

This website is created as a basic web development project using HTML and CSS to demonstrate the
fundamental concepts of website design and navigation.

Our Features

  • View academic programmes
  • Access the Student Corner
  • Learn about the institution
  • Simple and easy navigation
  • Beginner-friendly website design
